## Provenance

All records derive from the openFDA drug enforcement bulk export, a US federal public-domain dataset. `events.jsonl` aggregates 17,899 product-line records into 4,652 recall events by `event_id`. `events_labeled.jsonl` adds the 15-class defect taxonomy (96.1% rule-labeled, remainder by classifier). `benchmark.jsonl` holds 171 questions with gold evidence sets defined by structured predicates over corpus fields.

## Known limitations

`results/proposed_results.json` reports the recovered fraction twice, computed two ways:
`fraction_of_matched_control_recovered` (0.5318) from the main run, and the
`pool_sensitivity` row at pool = 300 (0.5317), where lambda is re-selected on dev at every
pool size and at that size lands on a neighbouring grid point. Both round to 0.53, the only
form either takes in the paper; `scripts/08_verify_manuscript.py` bounds the gap so it
cannot widen unnoticed.

Relevance is predicate-defined, which is the paper's subject: the benchmark cannot fairly evaluate a system with access to the same structured fields, because such a system can reconstruct the answer key. Gold sets for the 3.9% of events labeled by classifier inherit that classifier's errors. Recall records are the visible tail of quality events, not a population sample.
